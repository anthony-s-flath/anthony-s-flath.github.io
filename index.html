I am interested in cryptography, particularly lattice-based cryptography, fully homomorphic encryption, and the theory and construction of cryptographic systems.

I studied computer science at the University of Michigan. My research has included anonymous communication and lattice-based cryptography, alongside earlier software for scientific data collection.
