Selected work

CHARTS

CHARTS: Compute-Efficient Anonymous Routing through Tag Sums began as my undergraduate honors thesis and subsequently developed into a full paper, which has been submitted for publication. It explores an efficient anonymous routing scheme where anonymity is guaranteed through one honest-but-curious router and, to our knowledge, achieves the fastest known performance among schemes in this setting. This work was in collaboration with Ke Wu.

RWS-Lite

A low-cost platform for collecting weather and radiation measurements and serving the resulting data locally and online. This project was done with the Radiological Health Engineering Lab at the University of Michigan.
